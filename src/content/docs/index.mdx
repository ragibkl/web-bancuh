## Why Bancuh DNS

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Family-safe by default" icon="approve-check">
    Ads, trackers, malware, phishing and scams are blocked, along with adult
    content, gambling and dating sites. SafeSearch is enforced on Google, Bing,
    DuckDuckGo and Brave Search. [See what gets blocked](/filtering/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free, no strings" icon="heart">
    No sign-up, no subscription and no ads of its own. The servers are paid for
    out of pocket and will stay free for as long as possible.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One setting, every device" icon="laptop">
    Set it on your Wi-Fi router and everything on the network is covered,
    including smart TVs and game consoles where you can't install an ad
    blocker.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Encrypted if you want it" icon="seti:lock">
    Every server speaks plain DNS, DNS-over-TLS and DNS-over-HTTPS, so phones
    and laptops stay protected on any network, including mobile data.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Always up to date" icon="random">
    The blocklist is rebuilt every day from dozens of community-maintained
    sources. You never have to update anything.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open source" icon="github">
    The DNS server, the proxy in front of it, the blocklist sources and this
    website are all [on GitHub](https://github.com/ragibkl/adblock-dns-server).
    You can run your own copy in a few minutes.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
