Every Bancuh server keeps the DNS lookups it received from your IP address for
10 minutes. Use them to confirm you're connected, or to find out which domain a
broken app or website needs.

## Your recent lookups

This page asks every server, over IPv4 and over IPv6, for the lookups that came
from your address, and lists them together. It updates every few seconds while
it's open.

The page shows your IP address as the server sees it, then each lookup's time,
the name looked up, and the answer. An answer of `0.0.0.0` or `::` means it was
[blocked](/filtering/).

## Why is my log empty?

The page only shows lookups that came from **the same IP address your browser
is using now**. If they don't match, the page is empty even though Bancuh DNS
is working:

- **IPv4 vs IPv6.** Your router may send DNS over IPv4 while your browser
  opens the page over IPv6, or the other way round. The list at the top of this
  page checks both for you. On a single server's page, the **Auto** link lets
  your browser choose. The **IPv4 only** and **IPv6 only** links force one, so
  try both. If your network has no IPv6, the **IPv6 only** link won't open at
  all, and your browser may say the server can't be found.
- **VPNs and proxies.** If your browser goes through a VPN, iCloud Private
  Relay or a proxy, the page sees that address instead of yours.
- **Nothing recent.** Only the last 10 minutes are kept. Browse a little, then
  reload.
- **Wrong server.** Open the logs on the same server you set up.

## Privacy

- **Your lookups are visible only to you.** The logs page shows each visitor
  only the lookups made from their own IP address.
- **They're kept for 10 minutes.** Query logs are held in memory, not written
  to disk, and are dropped after 10 minutes.
- **Your lookups aren't sent to Google or Cloudflare.** Each server resolves
  names itself, asking the websites' own DNS servers directly, with DNSSEC
  validation.
- **Service logs.** For troubleshooting, each server also keeps about an hour
  of its own service logs, which can include IP addresses. These are rotated
  out automatically.
- **No accounts, no tracking.** Bancuh DNS doesn't ask who you are, and this
  website has no analytics or cookies.

Encrypted DNS (DoT or DoH) keeps your lookups private from your network
provider and anyone else on the network. Plain DNS doesn't.
